## Notable trends

**Fastest growing (Jul-Sep vs Apr-Jun, models with at least 15k units over six months):**

- Hyundai i20: 12,201 to 17,329 (+42%)
- Maruti Suzuki Celerio: 6,575 to 9,332 (+42%)
- Hyundai Creta: 37,694 to 52,033 (+38%)
- Tata Motors Tiago: 16,995 to 23,009 (+35%)
- Toyota Fortuner: 7,672 to 9,878 (+29%)
- Hyundai Aura: 15,641 to 20,032 (+28%)
- Mahindra XEV 9S: 10,762 to 13,275 (+23%)
- Hyundai Grand i10 Nios: 11,934 to 14,627 (+23%)

**Biggest decliners (same test):**

- Toyota Glanza: 10,070 to 6,130 (-39%)
- Toyota Urban Cruiser Hyryder: 26,883 to 22,536 (-16%)
- Maruti Suzuki Dzire: 66,025 to 59,963 (-9%)
- Kia Sonet: 29,475 to 27,454 (-7%)
- Maruti Suzuki Grand Vitara: 26,176 to 24,448 (-7%)

**September 2026 year-on-year standouts** (Auto Punditz top-25 table): Mahindra Bolero +253%, Maruti Victoris +208% (launched Sep 2025), Kia Seltos +117% (new generation), Maruti Baleno +93% (facelift; No. 1 in Sep), Maruti Alto +84%. Decliners: Mahindra Thar -24%, Toyota Innova -18%, Maruti Dzire -11%, Tata Nexon -9%.

**EV share:** dedicated EV nameplates (Mahindra XEV 9S, XEV 9e, BE 6, Maruti e Vitara from July, Toyota Ebella, Hyundai IONIQ 5) total 48,298 units, 1.8% of the market. That is a floor, not the real EV share: EV variants sold under ICE nameplates (Tata Nexon.ev, Punch.ev, Tiago.ev, Curvv.ev, Harrier.ev, Sierra EV; Hyundai Creta Electric; Mahindra XUV 3XO EV; Kia Carens Clavis EV, Syros EV) are counted inside their parent model, and MG (over 75-80% EV, including Windsor EV) has no model split. Rushlane reported Tata alone dispatched about 16.5k EVs in August 2026. The full EV share was not verified here.

**Body-type mix (share of model-level units tracked):**

- suv: 1,487,304 (57.5%)
- hatchback: 556,902 (21.5%)
- muv: 331,812 (12.8%)
- sedan: 209,216 (8.1%)
- pickup: 1,681 (0.1%)

Other points: Tata Motors was No. 2, ahead of Mahindra, in every month from April to September. The Tata Punch was the most consistent nameplate, at 19-21k units every month. Hyundai lost about 13,900 units of production in June after a supplier fire (Creta fell to 7,168), which inflates Creta's Q3-vs-Q2 growth. Maruti's Victoris is already a 10-14k-a-month SUV. New-launch volumes: Tata Sierra about 6-7k a month, Mahindra XEV 9S about 4k, Nissan Tekton fell from 1,670 to about 400 after its launch month.

## Caveats and what could not be verified

- All model-level numbers are **secondary** (Rushlane, Auto Punditz, Autocar India, MyCarJunction, MeroAuto), which compile OEM releases and SIAM data. Only brand totals for Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, Hyundai, Mahindra and Kia are OEM press-release figures. Cross-checks passed: Tata and Hyundai monthly models sum to the Rushlane Q2 totals; Kia and Mahindra Jul-Sep sum to the Q3 totals; every brand's model rows reconcile to its brand total within about 1%.
- `*` = month(s) missing. Maruti e Vitara Apr-Jun is inside Grand Vitara. Tata Aeris and Kia Sorento are new launches. Nissan Tekton launched in July. Toyota Ebella starts in May.
- Derived (arithmetic, not estimated): Kia July (Q3 minus Aug and Sep), Mahindra XEV 9e April, Nissan Magnite July, Honda May brand total.
- Not ranked: MG models (no model-wise disclosure), Citroën and Jeep models, BYD, VinFast, Tesla and luxury brands (no monthly wholesale reporting). Citroën and Jeep brand totals are missing for Apr, Jul and Aug.
- Source conflicts: Maruti Wagon R July (23,338 vs 23,398); Mahindra Bolero Aug (7,777 excl. Neo vs 8,159 incl.); Toyota May (30,574 vs 30,586). Autocar India quoted Maruti Sep as 1,68,744 in one article, but the Maruti model table and Rushlane both give 1,81,838, which is used here.
- SIAM's own monthly release gives segment totals, not models, and FADA retail is brand-level only. Neither was used for model ranks.
